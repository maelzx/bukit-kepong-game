      HISTORICAL NOTE

      On 23 February 1950, the police station at Bukit Kepong in Muar, Johor, was attacked before dawn by communist insurgents during the Malayan Emergency. A small number of police personnel, together with family members and auxiliaries present at the station, resisted the assault. The station was overrun and burnt, and many of its defenders were killed. The events are commemorated in Malaysia as an act of duty and resistance.

      This game is a dramatised interpretation, not a documentary reconstruction. The map layout, the wave structure, the ten-minute clock, the individual characters and every statistic in it are gameplay inventions. In particular, the "survive and hold" victory condition is a game objective and does not represent the historical outcome. For the actual history, consult published historical sources.
